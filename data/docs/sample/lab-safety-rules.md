## General Rules

All students must complete the online laboratory safety course and pass its quiz with a score of at least 80% before their first laboratory session. The certificate is valid for one academic year.

Food and drinks are strictly prohibited in all laboratories. Closed-toe shoes are required at all times. Long hair must be tied back when working near rotating machinery.

## Electronics Laboratory

Students must never work on live circuits above 50 volts without a teaching assistant present. Soldering irons must be returned to their stands immediately after use and switched off at the end of the session. Any damaged power cable or burnt component smell must be reported to the lab supervisor right away.

Each workbench is equipped with an emergency power cut-off switch, marked with a red label, located on the right side of the bench.

## Chemistry and Materials Laboratory

Safety goggles and lab coats are mandatory. Chemical waste must be disposed of in the labeled containers and never poured into the sink. Eye wash stations are located next to each exit door.

## Emergencies

In case of fire, activate the nearest fire alarm, leave the building using the marked emergency exits and gather at the assembly point in the main parking lot. Do not use elevators. Fire extinguishers are located at both ends of every corridor.

For injuries, contact the campus clinic at extension 2222. The clinic is open 24 hours a day during the semester.

## Equipment Borrowing

Students may borrow equipment such as oscilloscopes, multimeters and development boards for up to 7 days by presenting their student ID at the equipment desk. Late returns are charged 50 EGP per day, and students with overdue items cannot borrow again until they are returned.
