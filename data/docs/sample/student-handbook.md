## 1. Registration and Credit Hours

Students register for courses during the two-week registration window that opens three weeks before each semester. A regular semester load is between 12 and 18 credit hours. Students with a cumulative GPA of 3.5 or higher may request an overload of up to 21 credit hours with approval from their academic advisor. Students on academic probation are limited to a maximum of 12 credit hours.

Late registration is permitted during the first week of classes for a fee of 500 EGP. No registrations are accepted after the first week.

## 2. Adding and Dropping Courses

Courses may be added until the end of the second week of the semester. A course dropped before the end of the fourth week does not appear on the transcript. After the fourth week and until the end of the tenth week, a student may withdraw from a course and receive a grade of "W", which does not affect the GPA. Withdrawals are not permitted after the tenth week.

## 3. Grading System

Grades are awarded on a 4.0 scale: A = 4.0, A- = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, B = 3.0, B- = 2.7, C+ = 2.3, C = 2.0, C- = 1.7, D+ = 1.3, D = 1.0 and F = 0.0. The minimum passing grade for a course is D. However, a grade of C or higher is required in any course that is a prerequisite for another course in the student's major.

A student may repeat a course in which they received a grade lower than C. Only the higher of the two grades is counted in the cumulative GPA, but both attempts remain on the transcript. A course may be repeated at most twice.

## 4. Academic Standing and Probation

A student whose cumulative GPA falls below 2.0 is placed on academic probation. A student on probation must raise their cumulative GPA to 2.0 within two consecutive regular semesters. Failure to do so results in academic dismissal. Dismissed students may appeal to the Academic Affairs Committee within 14 days of the dismissal notice.

The Dean's Honor List recognizes students who complete at least 15 credit hours in a semester with a semester GPA of 3.7 or higher and no grade below B.

## 5. Attendance

Attendance is mandatory for all lectures, tutorials and laboratory sessions. A student whose absence exceeds 25% of the scheduled sessions of a course without an accepted excuse receives a warning and is barred from sitting the final exam, resulting in a grade of F. Medical excuses must be submitted to the Student Affairs Office within 7 days of the absence.

## 6. Examinations

The final exam counts for 40% of the course grade unless the course syllabus states otherwise. The remaining 60% is distributed between midterm exams, assignments, quizzes and projects. Students who miss a final exam with an accepted excuse may sit a make-up exam, which is held during the first two weeks of the following semester.

Any form of cheating in an examination results in a grade of F in the course and the case is referred to the Disciplinary Board. A second offense results in suspension for one full semester.

## 7. Graduation Requirements

To graduate with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering, a student must complete 160 credit hours with a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0. This includes a two-semester graduation project worth 6 credit hours and a summer industrial training of at least 8 weeks. Students graduate with honors if their cumulative GPA is 3.5 or higher and they have never been on academic probation.

## 8. Contact

The Student Affairs Office is located in Building B, room 104, and is open Sunday to Thursday from 9:00 to 15:00.
